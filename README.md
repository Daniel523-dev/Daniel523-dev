# About Me

I am a Python developer focused on cybersecurity. I have been using Python since 2018. Have spent the past few years working in the cybersecurity field.

# My Philosophy

I believe in open-source software and licenses such as **GPL-3.0**.

I disagree with the blanket advice to "don't roll your crypto" because I believe that hinders innovation. Everyone has the right and responsibility to innovate in their field. However no one is perfect. Our work will always have room for improvement. We should never let "the best I can do" become an excuse to stop growing. Instead we should never stop learning, challenging ourselves and striving to do better than our best.

I believe the best way to create something is to **learn by doing.**
