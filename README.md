# About Me

I am a Python developer focused on cybersecurity. I have been using Python since 2018 and have spent the past few years working in the cybersecurity field (as a hobby).

# My Philosophy

I believe in open-source software and licenses such as **GPL-3.0**.

I disagree with the blanket advice to "don't roll your crypto" because I believe that hinders innovation. I believe everyone has the right and responsibility to innovate in their field. However no one is perfect. Our work will always have room for improvement. We should never let "the best I can do" become an excuse to stop growing. Instead we should never stop learning, challenging ourselves and striving to do better than our best.

I believe the best way to create something great is to **learn by doing** over many projects, and let's face it, the first several dozen projects you or I ever make/made will probably suck, and that's not just ok, it's normal. But every once in a while, we will create a gem. However, given that no one is perfect, we should always revisit even our best projects and make a few revisions.
